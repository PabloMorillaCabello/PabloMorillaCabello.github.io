04 — Publications

In print

Conference paper
SPS 2024

Exploring the Synergies of Modularization, Interface Standardization, and Service-Orientation in Production System Simulation

M. Birtic, P. Morilla Cabello, Á. Muñoz, A. Syberfeldt

Advances in Transdisciplinary Engineering, Vol. 52, pp. 185–195 · DOI 10.3233/ATDE240164 ↗

The paper investigates a combination of concepts that promote reconfigurability and flexibility in production systems. By integrating modularization, interface standardization and a service-oriented architecture, it explores whether these synergies can improve the simulation of production systems in constant flux — enabling faster, easier creation and updating of digital models.
